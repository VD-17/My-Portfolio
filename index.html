About Me

Inquisitive Software Developer

I am a curiosity-driven software developer with a strong builder's mindset, focused on backend, web, and mobile application development. My journey into technology began with a deep curiosity about how computers and digital devices work. This curiosity pushed me to constantly explore new technologies and ideas, eventually motivating me to pursue IT so I could not only understand systems, but also build them.

I am driven by the desire to bring ideas to life through technology, whether in AI, cloud computing, web platforms, or mobile applications. I believe that building small projects is a powerful way to grow, learn, and develop unique solutions, and I continuously explore new tools and concepts to sharpen my skills.

I have built projects such as a queue management system, a C2C eCommerce website, and a stateful chatbot. These experiences have given me a comprehensive understanding of system design, user-centered development, and scalable solutions, and they continue to fuel my ambition to create more impactful and innovative software.

With hands-on experience in Python, Android Studio, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, SQL, and MongoDB, I focus on creating practical, efficient, and user-friendly solutions. I am always looking to refine my skills, explore emerging technologies, and contribute to projects that solve real problems and push my technical boundaries.
